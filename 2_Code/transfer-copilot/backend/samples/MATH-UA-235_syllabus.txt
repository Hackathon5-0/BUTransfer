MATH-UA 235 Probability & Statistics

Course description:
Probability and statistics: probability models, estimation and hypothesis testing.

Learning outcomes:
Perform estimation and testing.

Weekly schedule:
Week 1-2 Probability axioms, counting, conditional probability. Week 3-4 Discrete and continuous random variables. Week 5 Expectation and variance. Week 6 Midterm. Week 7 Sampling distributions and the central limit theorem. Week 8-9 Point estimation and confidence intervals. Week 10-11 Hypothesis testing. Week 12-13 Simple linear regression. Week 14 Review. Assessment: problem sets 30%, midterm 30%, final 40%.
